2019

Evopump Firmware

2019

Development firmware for CamMED's Evopump, an electrolysis-driven insulin patch pump, on the Nordic nRF52840. It controls the voltage and current that drive electrolysis, reads temperature and pressure for environmental compensation, and runs a real-time dosing timetable that adjusts based on how much medication has already been delivered. Elapsed time is kept on a low-frequency clock and saved to flash every five seconds to survive power loss, and logged data is sent over Bluetooth Low Energy to an iOS app. An ADC monitors battery voltage.

  • C
  • nRF52840
  • BLE
  • Medical Devices
  • Embedded
